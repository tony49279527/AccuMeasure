**AccuMeasure** is a brand/company name, and "ISO 9001 certified factory" means its manufacturing facility has been certified under the **ISO 9001** quality management standard. Here's what that typically means:

## What ISO 9001 Certification Means
- **ISO 9001** is an international standard for **Quality Management Systems (QMS)**, published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO).
- Certification is issued by an accredited third-party body after an audit, not by ISO itself.
- It confirms the factory has documented processes for quality control, consistency, traceability, and **continuous improvement**.

## What It Indicates About the Factory
- Documented procedures for design, production, inspection, and corrective action
- Regular internal and external audits
- Management commitment to meeting customer and regulatory requirements
- A framework for reducing defects and improving consistency

## What It Does *Not* Guarantee
- It does **not** certify a specific product's quality or safety (that's what standards like CE, UL, RoHS, or ISO 13485 are for)
- It doesn't mean every unit is defect-free
- It doesn't verify the accuracy of marketing claims

## If You're Evaluating This Supplier
You may want to:
1. Ask for the **certificate** and check the **certification body** (e.g., TÜV, SGS, BSI, Bureau Veritas) and its accreditation
2. Verify the certificate on the body's website (many have online validity checkers)
3. Check the **scope** of certification — does it cover the actual products/factory you're buying from?
4. Confirm the certificate's **expiry date** and whether it's current
