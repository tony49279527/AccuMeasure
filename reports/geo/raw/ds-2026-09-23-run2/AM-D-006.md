| MOQ | Typically 500–2,000 units for private label |

## Private Label Requirements From Manufacturer

- **Logo printing** on gauge body, dial face, or packaging
- **Custom packaging** (blister, color box, gift box)
- **Custom color** housing (Pantone matching)
- **Manual/branding inserts**
- **Barcode/UPC** labeling
- **Custom firmware** (units, auto-off timing, backlight)

## Regional Alternatives

- **Taiwan**: Tecsis, Kuen Long — higher quality, higher MOQ
- **India**: H Guru, Wika India — domestic private label
- **USA**: Winters Instruments, Ashcroft — mostly rebranding, high MOQ
- **Europe**: Wika, Keller — premium OEM partnerships only

## Recommended Sourcing Approach

1. Shortlist 5–8 suppliers on Alibaba/Made-in-China with "verified" and "OEM/ODM" tags
2. Request samples (usually $20–60 each, refundable on order)
3. Confirm certifications (CE/RoHS essential for export)
4. Negotiate MOQ — many will do 500–1,000 units for first order
5. Use a trading agent or inspection service (SGS, QIMA) for QC
