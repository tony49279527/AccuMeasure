Importing radar level transmitters involves several technical, regulatory, and commercial considerations. Here's a comprehensive checklist:

## Technical Specifications
- **Measurement range** – Confirm it matches your tank/vessel height and required accuracy
- **Frequency band** – 6 GHz, 26 GHz, 80 GHz, etc. (higher frequency = better for narrow tanks, foams, solids)
- **Process conditions** – Temperature, pressure, dielectric constant of the medium
- **Output signal** – 4-20 mA HART, Modbus, Profibus, Foundation Fieldbus, etc.
- **Power supply** – 2-wire loop-powered, 4-wire, or 24V DC
- **Antenna type** – Horn, rod, lens, or parabolic (depends on application)
- **Ingress protection** – IP66/IP67/IP68 rating
- **Ex certification** – ATEX, IECEx, NEC/CEC if hazardous area
- **Accuracy & repeatability** – Typically ±3mm to ±10mm

## Regulatory & Compliance
- **Country certifications** – FCC (US), CE (EU), IC (Canada), MIC (Japan), etc.
- **Radio type approval** – Radar uses ISM bands; must be approved for the destination country
- **Hazardous area certs** – Match local requirements (ATEX, IECEx, UL, CSA)
- **RoHS/REACH** compliance for EU
- **Pressure equipment directive** (PED) if applicable

## Supplier Verification
- **Manufacturer authenticity** – Verify they're an authorized distributor or OEM (beware counterfeits)
- **ISO 9001 certification**
- **Reference projects / case studies**
- **Warranty terms** – Typically 12–24 months
- **After-sales support** – Local service, spare parts availability, calibration services
- **Lead time & MOQ**

## Commercial Terms
- **Incoterms** (FOB, CIF, DDP, etc.)
- **Payment terms** – L/C, T/T, escrow
- **HS code & tariff/duty rates** for your country
- **Shipping insurance**
- **Customs documentation** – Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, test reports

## Pre-Shipment Quality Checks
- **Factory Acceptance Test (FAT)** report
- **Calibration certificate** traceable to national standards
- **Serial numbers & nameplate** verification
- **Packaging inspection** – Shock, moisture protection, foam-lined cases
- **Sample unit testing** before bulk order

## Application-Specific Considerations
- **Foam, vapor, or condensation** – May need guided wave radar instead
- **Solids vs. liquids** – Different antenna and algorithms
- **Agitators, baffles, or internal structures** – Can cause false echoes
- **Low dielectric media** (<2) – Requires special tuning
- **Interface measurement** (two liquids) – Check if model supports it

## Post-Import
- **Local calibration/verification** if required by regulations
- **Configuration software** availability (e.g., HART communicator, PC tools)
- **Spare parts & consumables** (gaskets, seals, antennas)

---

**Bottom line:** Don't rely solely on the datasheet. Request FAT reports, verify certifications for *your* country, test a sample unit, and confirm the supplier supports calibration and spares long-term. A cheap unit that fails certification or calibration costs far more than a compliant one.
