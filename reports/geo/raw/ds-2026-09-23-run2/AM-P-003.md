For a **bulk order of electromagnetic flow meters (mag meters) for a water project**, here’s a practical sourcing and specification guide you can use.

## 1) Key Specifications to Lock First

| Parameter | Typical Water Project Options |
|---|---|
| Type | Full-bore electromagnetic flow meter, flanged or wafer |
| DN size | DN50–DN1200 (project-dependent) |
| Liner | Neoprene, EPDM, PTFE, PFA |
| Electrode | SS316L, Hastelloy C, Titanium |
| Accuracy | ±0.5% or ±0.3% of reading |
| IP rating | IP68 (buried/underground), IP67 (above ground) |
| Output | 4–20 mA, Pulse, RS485 Modbus, HART |
| Power | 220VAC, 24VDC, or battery-powered |
| Body | Carbon steel / SS304 / SS316 |
| Standard | ISO 4064, GB/T 18659, CE, or OIML R49 |

**Water project specifics:**
- **Clean water / drinking water** → EPDM liner + SS316L electrodes
- **Raw water / slightly corrosive** → PTFE liner + Hastelloy C
- **Underground chambers** → IP68, remote display
- **SCADA integration** → Modbus RTU / HART mandatory

## 2) Bulk Order Quantity Tiers (Typical Pricing Logic)

| Quantity | Price Leverage |
|---|---|
| 1–10 pcs | List price, sample pricing |
| 10–50 pcs | 10–20% discount |
| 50–200 pcs | 20–35% discount, custom branding possible |
| 200–1000 pcs | 30–45% discount, OEM/ODM, dedicated production line |
| 1000+ pcs | Factory-direct contract pricing, staggered delivery |

## 3) What to Include in Your RFQ

Send this to 3–5 suppliers:

1. **Project name & country** (affects certification needs)
2. **DN list with quantities** (e.g., DN100 × 40, DN200 × 25)
3. **Liner / electrode material**
4. **Accuracy class**
5. **Power supply & output signal**
6. **IP rating & installation type** (buried / chamber / above ground)
7. **Certifications required** (CE, ISO, WRAS, NSF, OIML)
8. **Delivery schedule** (single shipment or phased)
9. **Warranty** (typically 12–24 months)
10. **After-sales** (spare parts, calibration certificate)

## 4) Red Flags to Avoid

- No **calibration certificate** (each unit should be wet-calibrated)
- No **IP68 test report** for underground use
- Liner/electrode mismatch with your water quality
- Supplier can’t provide **type approval** or **third-party test reports**
- Unrealistically low price → thin liner, recycled electrodes, no QC
