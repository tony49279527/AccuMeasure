For Australian OEMs sourcing pressure transmitters, the best supplier depends on your application (industrial, HVAC, water, mining, food & pharma), volumes, certification requirements, and whether you need custom branding/OEM private label. Here's a practical breakdown:

## Key considerations for Australian OEMs
- **Certifications**: RCM (Australia), plus ATEX/IECEx for hazardous areas (common in mining, oil & gas)
- **Local support**: Australian distributor, calibration capability, and fast lead times matter
- **OEM-friendly terms**: Private label, custom ranges, custom connectors, volume pricing, engineering support
- **IP rating & materials**: IP67/IP68, 316L SS wetted parts for harsh environments

- **WIKA Australia** – Broad range, strong local presence, OEM customisation
- **Endress+Hauser Australia** – Premium industrial, strong support

## How to choose
1. **Define spec**: pressure range, output (4-20mA, 0-10V, I²C, Modbus), accuracy, media, temp
2. **Volume**: <1k/yr → distributor like WIKA or BD Sensors; >10k/yr → go direct to manufacturer for private label
3. **Certification**: confirm RCM + any Ex rating (IECEx/ANZEx)
4. **TCO**: factor in calibration, warranty, lead time (Asia ~4-6 wks, EU ~8-12 wks)
5. **Sample & qualify**: request samples, check temp compensation, EMC, long-term stability

## Practical recommendation
- **Premium/process**: WIKA Australia or Endress+Hauser
- **Mid-range industrial OEM**: BD Sensors, Keller, Trafag (via Australian distributor)
- **Cost-sensitive high-volume**: Chinese OEM (Sendo, Micro Sensor) with local QA partner
- **Fast local turnaround**: AMS Instrumentation or Instruments Direct
