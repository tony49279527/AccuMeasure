## Selection Criteria for Chemical & Water Storage

| Factor | What to Check |
|---|---|
| **Material compatibility** | PTFE, PFA, PVDF, 316SS, Hastelloy for aggressive chemicals |
| **Probe type** | Rod, cable, or fully insulated for conductive liquids |
| **Approvals** | ATEX, IECEx, FM, CSA for hazardous areas |
| **Output** | 4–20 mA, HART, Modbus, relay, IO-Link |
| **Temperature/pressure** | Match to tank conditions |
| **Conductivity** | Capacitive works well for both conductive and non-conductive liquids, but tuning differs |
| **Hygiene** | 3-A, EHEDG for food/pharma |

## Practical Tips

- For **water storage**, standard 316SS or PVC-insulated probes are usually fine.
- For **aggressive chemicals**, specify PTFE/PFA-insulated probes and chemically resistant housings.
- For **foaming or coating liquids**, capacitive sensors can struggle — consider guided wave radar as an alternative.
- Always confirm **wetted materials** against your chemical's compatibility chart (e.g., Cole-Parmer or manufacturer data).
