For OEM air compressor panels, a digital pressure gauge needs to balance accuracy, durability, cost, and integration simplicity. Here's a practical breakdown:

## Key Specs to Look For

**Pressure Range**
- Typical compressor systems: 0–200 psi (0–14 bar) for reciprocating; 0–350 psi for some rotary screw
- Choose range ~1.5–2× max working pressure for headroom
- Common: 0–150, 0–200, 0–300 psi

**Accuracy**
- ±0.5% FS is standard for OEM panel use
- ±0.25% FS if used for control/feedback loops

**Output Signal** (critical for OEM integration)
- **4–20 mA** – most common for PLC/controller input
- **0–10 V** – simple, cheap, good for short runs
- **RS-485 / Modbus RTU** – if panel has digital comms
- **Switch outputs (NPN/PNP)** – for direct load/unload control
- Some gauges combine display + 4–20 mA + relays (e.g., IFM PN series, SMC ISE/ZSE)

**Process Connection**
- 1/4" NPT, 1/8" NPT, or G1/4 (BSPP for export)
- 316L wetted parts for oil-laden air

**Environment / Panel Constraints**
- IP65 front-face minimum (IP67 for washdown)
- Operating temp: -10 to 60 °C
- Panel cutout: 1/8 DIN (22 mm) or 1/4 DIN typical
- 2-wire loop-powered (no external supply needed) is a big plus for panels

## Design Considerations for Panels

1. **Loop power vs. 3-wire**: 2-wire 4–20 mA gauges simplify wiring and share the PLC input — preferred when possible.
2. **Snubber or damping**: Add a snubber to protect the sensor from compressor pulsation (high-cycle fatigue kills sensors fast).
3. **Condensate/water traps**: Mount vertical or use a siphon so oil/water doesn't pool on the sensor.
4. **Display update rate**: Look for adjustable averaging to smooth pulsation readings.
5. **Ratings**: For outdoor/TEFC panels, verify UL/CSA or CE depending on market.
6. **Calibration**: Choose models with NIST-traceable cert if end-customer requires it.

## Questions to Nail Down Before Selecting
1. Signal output the panel controller accepts (4–20 mA? Modbus?)
2. Required accuracy vs. cost target
3. Pulsation/vibration environment (recip vs. screw)
4. Panel cutout size and bezel style
5. Certifications required (UL, CE, ATEX if hazardous)
6. Volume and annual usage (drives whether to go direct to Asian mfr or distributor)
