## What to Vet for EU Private Label

| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| **CE marking + EU Declaration of Conformity** | Legal requirement |
| **ATEX/IECEx** (if hazardous area) | Explosive atmospheres |
| **RoHS / REACH** | Material compliance |
| **EN 61010 / EN 60947** | Electrical safety |
| **WEEE registration** | If you're the importer |
| **EU Authorised Rep** | Required if manufacturer is non-EU |
| **MOQ ≤ 500–1000 pcs** | Private label viability |
| **Custom housing/branding** | Laser marking, custom labels, connectors |
| **Lead time ≤ 6–8 weeks** | Retail replenishment |
| **PPAP / sample program** | Quality assurance |

## Suggested Sourcing Strategy

1. **Define spec first** – medium, temp, pressure, output (4-20mA, reed, NPN), IP rating, certs needed.
2. **Shortlist 3–5** from Tier 2 + Tier 3 depending on volume and price point.
3. **Request**: datasheet, CE DoC, ATEX cert, MOQ, tooling cost, sample lead time, private-label terms.
4. **Use EU Authorised Rep** if sourcing from China/Taiwan (mandatory under EU MDR/ATEX/EMC).
5. **Pilot order** – 100–500 units with your branding before scaling.
