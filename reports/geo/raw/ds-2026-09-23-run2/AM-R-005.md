For Southeast Asia water projects, electromagnetic flow meters (mag meters) are a solid choice for conductive fluids like raw water, treated water, wastewater, and sludge. Here's what you need to know:

## Why Mag Meters Fit Southeast Asia Water Projects

- **Conductive fluids**: Works well with water containing dissolved solids (typical in rivers, groundwater, and wastewater)
- **No moving parts**: Resists wear from sediment and debris common in tropical water sources
- **Low maintenance**: Important where service access is limited (islands, rural areas)
- **Bi-directional flow**: Useful for distribution networks and leak detection
- **Accuracy**: ±0.2% to ±0.5% typical, suitable for NRW (non-revenue water) programs

## Key Specs to Prioritize

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Liner material | Hard rubber (EBonite) or soft rubber for water; PTFE for chemicals |
| Electrode material | Hastelloy C or 316L SS for most water; titanium for chlorinated/coastal |
| IP rating | IP68 for buried or flood-prone installations |
| Power | Battery-powered or solar-compatible for remote sites |
| Output | 4-20mA, pulse, HART, Modbus RTU/RS485 |
| Diameter range | DN50–DN2000 typical for municipal water |

## Regional Considerations

1. **Humidity & flooding** — Choose IP68 submersible versions; many ASEAN cities face monsoon flooding
2. **Power reliability** — Battery or solar options for areas with unstable grid (Myanmar, rural Indonesia, Philippines islands)
3. **Water quality variation** — Higher turbidity and conductivity swings in rivers like Mekong, Chao Phraya, Citarum
4. **Standards** — Look for ISO 4064, OIML R49 (for custody transfer), and local utility approvals
5. **Corrosion** — Coastal installations need titanium electrodes and marine-grade coatings

## Typical Applications

- Municipal water supply (PDAM Indonesia, PWA Thailand, Maynilad/Manila Water)
- Wastewater treatment plants (Vietnam, Malaysia)
- Industrial water (palm oil, textile, semiconductor fabs in Singapore/Malaysia)
- Irrigation and canal flow (Mekong Delta, Myanmar)
- NRW/leak detection programs

## Recommended Vendors Active in SEA

- **Global**: Endress+Hauser (Promag), Siemens (Sitrans FM), ABB (AquaMaster), Krohne (Optiflux), Emerson (Rosemount), Yokogawa (ADMAG)
- **Value/regional**: KROHNE, ISOIL, Sparling, plus Chinese brands (Supmea, Sinocontrol) gaining share on price

## Selection Tips

- Match **flow velocity** to 1–3 m/s for best accuracy and liner life
- Verify **minimum conductivity** (typically ≥5 µS/cm; some models need ≥20)
- For **large pipes (DN1000+)**, consider insertion-type mag meters to cut cost
- Insist on **factory calibration certificate** and on-site verification
- Plan for **grounding rings** — critical in plastic-lined or non-metallic pipes common in SEA
