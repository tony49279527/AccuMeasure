## Selection Tips for Lab vs. Industrial

| Consideration | Laboratory | Industrial/Process |
|---|---|---|
| Flow range | µL/min to low SLPM | SLPM to hundreds of SLPM |
| Accuracy | ±0.5–1% of reading | ±1–2% of full scale |
| Turndown | 50:1 to 200:1 | 50:1 to 100:1 |
| Gas calibration | Multi-gas / actual gas | Air/N₂ default |
| Output | Analog + digital (RS-232/485, Modbus) | 4–20 mA, HART, Modbus |
| Wetted materials | Stainless steel, PTFE, Viton | 316L SS typical |
