Here's a practical guide to shipping pressure transmitter samples via DHL:

## 1. Packaging Requirements

**Box & Cushioning**
- Use a rigid double-wall cardboard box (or original manufacturer packaging if available)
- Wrap each transmitter in anti-static bubble wrap or foam
- Use custom foam inserts if shipping multiple units to prevent movement
- Fill void space to eliminate shifting during transit

**Thread & Port Protection**
- Cap all process connections (G1/2, 1/4" NPT, etc.) with plastic thread protectors
- Seal pressure ports with plugs to prevent debris ingress
- Cover electrical connectors (M12, DIN 43650, cable glands)

**Moisture Protection**
- Add silica gel desiccant packets
- Wrap in VCI (vapor corrosion inhibitor) film for international shipments

## 2. Documentation

| Document | When Needed |
|---|---|
| Commercial Invoice | All international shipments |
| Proforma Invoice | Samples with no commercial value |
| Packing List | Multiple items |
| Certificate of Origin | Upon customs request |
| MSDS/SDS | If containing oils, fill fluids, or batteries |
| CE/ATEX Declaration | For hazardous area models |

**For samples**, mark the invoice clearly:
- "Sample – Not for resale – No commercial value" (declare a nominal value like USD 5–20 to satisfy customs)
- Include HS Code: typically **9026.20** (instruments for measuring pressure)

## 3. DHL Service Options

- **DHL Express Worldwide** – Best for samples, 1–5 business days
- **DHL Express Easy** – Flat-rate for small parcels, up to 5 kg
- **DHL Medical Express** – Not applicable unless specified
- Request **signature required** and **declared value insurance** (transmitters can be costly)

## 4. Restricted Items Check

⚠️ Pressure transmitters may be flagged if they:
- Contain **lithium batteries** (some smart transmitters) → need UN38.3 test report
- Contain **oil fill** (e.g., silicon oil in some models) → may need SDS
- Are **ATEX/IECEx certified** → documentation may be required
- Have **residual pressure or hazardous media** → must be purged and certified clean

## 5. Booking Tips

1. Use DHL's online portal or account for discounted rates
2. Declare accurate weight/dimensions — transmitters are dense
3. Choose **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** if you want recipient to avoid customs fees, or **DAP** if recipient handles duties
4. Insure for full replacement value
5. Take photos of packaging before sealing

## 6. Typical Labeling

- "FRAGILE – Handle with Care"
- "THIS SIDE UP" arrows
- "SENSITIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT"
- Recipient phone number (required by DHL for delivery)
