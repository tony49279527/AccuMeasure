Warranty terms for industrial pressure transmitters vary significantly by manufacturer, product tier, and market. Here's a general overview:

## Typical Warranty Periods

- **Standard industrial transmitters:** 12–24 months from date of shipment (most common)
- **Premium/branded transmitters** (e.g., Emerson Rosemount, Endress+Hauser, Yokogawa, Honeywell, Siemens): often **2–5 years**
- **Budget/generic models** (many Chinese or low-cost brands): typically **12 months**, sometimes 18 months
- **High-reliability/safety-rated models** (SIL-rated, nuclear, aerospace): can be **5–10 years** or full product lifecycle contracts

## Common Warranty Structures

| Type | Typical Coverage |
|------|------------------|
| **Time-based** | 12, 18, 24, or 36 months from shipment or installation |
| **Extended warranty** | Purchased add-on, often +2 to +5 years |
| **Lifetime/limited lifetime** | Some premium brands (e.g., certain Rosemount models) |
| **Pro-rated** | Coverage decreases in value over time |

## What's Usually Covered

- Manufacturing defects
- Material faults
- Performance outside published specifications under normal use

## What's Usually Excluded

- Improper installation, wiring, or power supply
- Over-pressure, over-temperature, or corrosion damage
- Media incompatibility (wrong wetted materials)
- Damage from surges, lightning, or water ingress
- Normal wear (diaphragm fatigue, seal degradation)
- Unauthorized repair or modification
- Consumables (O-rings, gaskets, fill fluid)

## Key Details to Check in Any Warranty

1. **Start date** – shipment vs. installation vs. commissioning (some allow deferred start if stored properly)
2. **Return process** – RMA required? Who pays freight?
3. **Repair vs. replace** – manufacturer's option, usually
4. **Turnaround time** – often not guaranteed
5. **Calibration drift** – usually NOT covered unless severe
6. **Transferability** – usually tied to original purchaser

## Notable Manufacturer Examples (approximate, verify current terms)

- **Rosemount (Emerson):** typically 1–5 years depending on series; some have 5-year limited warranty
- **Endress+Hauser:** generally 2 years, extendable
- **Yokogawa:** typically 1–2 years, longer on some lines
- **Honeywell:** 1–2 years standard
- **WIKA, VEGA, Siemens:** 1–3 years typical
- **Generic/import:** 12 months is standard

## Practical Advice

- **Get it in writing** on the PO/contract — don't rely on datasheets alone
- **Register the product** if required (some warranties require it)
- **Document installation conditions** — most claims are denied due to application issues
- **Consider total cost of ownership** — a 5-year warranty from a premium brand often beats a cheap 1-year unit for critical processes
- **For SIL/safety applications**, warranty terms are often negotiated separately
