# Importing Industrial Sensors from Xi'an, China

Xi'an is a significant hub for industrial automation and sensing technology, with companies producing pressure sensors, flow meters, temperature sensors, proximity sensors, and more. Here's a practical guide:

## 1. Identify Suppliers

**Platforms:**
- **Alibaba / Made-in-China / Global Sources** – search "Xi'an sensor supplier" or specific products
- **1688.com** – domestic Chinese wholesale (cheaper, but requires a Chinese agent)
- **Trade shows** – China International Industry Fair, Sensor+Test China

**Direct sourcing:**
- Look for manufacturers, not trading companies (verify via their business license)
- Check if they export already (better English, documentation, export experience)
- Request **ISO 9001, CE, ATEX, RoHS, or IECEx** certifications depending on your application

## 2. Verify the Supplier

- **Business license** (营业执照) – confirm scope and registered capital
- **Factory audit** – via third party (SGS, Bureau Veritas, TÜV) or video tour
- **Sample order first** – test quality before bulk
- **References** – ask for existing export customers
- **Cross-check** on multiple platforms

⚠️ Xi'an has legitimate manufacturers *and* trading companies posing as factories — verify carefully.

## 3. Negotiate Terms

- **Price** – FOB Shanghai/Tianjin or EXW is common; CIF if you want them to handle freight
- **MOQ** – often negotiable for first order
- **Payment** – typically 30% deposit / 70% before shipment; use **T/T via bank** or **LC** for large orders
- **Avoid** Western Union / personal accounts
- **Lead time** – usually 15–45 days

## 4. Compliance & Documentation

Required for import:
- **Commercial Invoice**
- **Packing List**
- **Bill of Lading / Airway Bill**
- **Certificate of Origin** (for tariff purposes)
- **Product certifications** (CE, FCC, etc. — depends on your country)
- **HS Code** – sensors typically fall under **9025, 9026, 9027, 8536, or 8543** — confirm the correct code for duty rates

## 5. Shipping

- **Samples/small orders:** DHL/FedEx/UPS (3–7 days)
- **Bulk:** Sea freight via forwarder (25–40 days) or air freight (5–10 days)
- **Incoterms:** FOB is safest for beginners
- **Insurance:** Always insure — sensors can be fragile

Consider a **freight forwarder** who handles customs clearance at destination.

## 6. Import Duties & Taxes

- Check your country's tariff for the specific HS code
- US: Section 301 tariffs may apply to Chinese electronics
- EU: VAT + duty on arrival
- Consider **de minimis** thresholds for samples

## 7. Common Pitfalls

- Fake certifications
- Wrong HS code → delays/seizures
- No pre-shipment inspection → quality surprises
- Paying full amount upfront
- Ignoring EMC/electrical standards in your country

## 8. Recommended Workflow

1. Shortlist 3–5 Xi'an suppliers
2. Request quotes + certifications
3. Order samples
4. Third-party inspection (QC) before final payment
5. Confirm HS code and duty with a customs broker
6. Ship via forwarder
7. Inspect on arrival
