Here are the main flow meter suppliers used by UK water utilities, grouped by category. The UK water sector is dominated by a handful of framework suppliers, especially for customer metering, while network/distribution flow measurement involves a wider set.

## Domestic / Customer Metering (AMR/AMI)

- **Sensus (Xylem)** – iPERL and allysonic meters; one of the most widely deployed in UK customer metering, major framework supplier.
- **Itron** – Widely used across UK utilities; strong in AMR/AMI and commercial/industrial metering.
- **Kamstrup** – Ultrasonic meters (flowIQ range); common with UK utilities moving to smart metering.
- **Diehl Metering** – Hydrus ultrasonic meters; active in UK water frameworks.
- **Honeywell (Elster)** – Long-established UK water metering presence (e.g., V200/V210).
- **Arad** – Increasing UK presence, especially in smart metering trials.
- **Maddalena** – Italian manufacturer with UK distribution, used in some utility and sub-metering applications.
- **Zenner** – Active in UK water and sub-metering.
- **Apator** – Polish manufacturer, some UK presence.
- **B Meter / Metron Farnier** – Niche UK players.

## Network / Distribution / Bulk Flow (Mag & Ultrasonic)

- **Krohne** – Electromagnetic and ultrasonic; widely specified for clean/wastewater networks.
- **Endress+Hauser** – Promag (mag) and Prosonic/Flowmaster (ultrasonic); common in treatment works.
- **ABB** – AquaMaster (mag) and ultrasonic; strong in leakage and district metering.
- **Siemens** – Sitrans MAG and ultrasonic ranges.
- **Sensus (Xylem)** – Also supplies network mag meters (e.g., M2000).
- **Badger Meter** – Mag and ultrasonic (Dynasonics) for water networks.
- **Flexim** – Clamp-on ultrasonic, popular for verification and retrofits.
- **Katronic** – UK-based clamp-on ultrasonic specialist.
- **Micronics** – UK clamp-on ultrasonic and fixed meters.
- **Pulsar Measurement** – UK brand (formerly Pulsar/Clarke) for open channel and ultrasonic.
- **Nivus** – Open-channel and clamp-on.

## Framework / Route to Market

Most UK water utilities buy through:

- **Frameworks** – e.g., those run by **Ofwat-approved** arrangements or the utilities' own alliances (e.g., United Utilities, Thames Water, Severn Trent, Anglian Water have their own supply chains often managed via **Achilles UVDB** pre-qualification).
- **Distributors / integrators** – e.g., **PMA (Process Measurement & Analysis)**, **Instrumatic**, **Alliance Instruments**, **RS Hydro** (also a Flexim/Katronic distributor), **Oleum**, and **Sentek**.
- **Direct OEM agreements** – Larger utilities often contract directly with Sensus, Itron, Kamstrup, Diehl for meter supply and AMI rollouts.

## Notes for Selection

- **Customer meters**: Ultrasonic (Kamstrup, Diehl, Sensus iPERL) is replacing mechanical for AMI rollouts.
- **DMAs / leakage**: Electromagnetic (ABB AquaMaster, Krohne, Sensus M2000) plus clamp-on ultrasonic for verification.
- **Wastewater / treatment**: Mag meters (Krohne, E+H, ABB) with appropriate liners/electrodes.
- **Open channel**: Pulsar, Nivus, ISCO/Teledyne.
- **Standards**: Look for **WRAS approval**, **MCERTS** where relevant, and **OIML R49 / MID** for customer meters.
