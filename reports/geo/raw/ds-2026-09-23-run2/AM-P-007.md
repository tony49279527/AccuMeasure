Bulk purchasing level sensors for remote tank monitoring involves several key considerations. Here's a practical guide:

## Key Selection Criteria

**Measurement Technology**
- **Ultrasonic** – Non-contact, good for liquids, affected by foam/vapor
- **Radar (24/80 GHz)** – Most reliable, works with vapors/foam, higher cost
- **Pressure/Submersible** – Best for deep tanks, needs cable entry
- **Float switches** – Simple on/off, not for continuous level
- **Capacitive/RF admittance** – Good for solids and viscous liquids

**Connectivity Options**
- NB-IoT / LTE-M – Low power, cellular, works where WiFi doesn't
- LoRaWAN – Private networks, long range, needs gateway
- Sigfox – Very low data, regional coverage
- WiFi/BLE – Short range, often for setup only
- RS-485/Modbus – Wired, for industrial integration

**Power**
- Battery (3–10 year life typical for NB-IoT/LoRa)
- Solar + rechargeable
- Loop-powered (4–20 mA)

## Bulk Purchase Considerations

| Factor | What to Check |
|---|---|
| MOQ | Usually 50–500 units for OEMs; distributors often lower |
| Unit price breaks | Typically at 100, 500, 1,000, 5,000+ |
| Certifications | FCC/CE, ATEX/IECEx if hazardous areas, IP67/68 |
| Platform | Vendor cloud vs. open (MQTT, HTTP API) |
| SIM/data | Bundled vs. BYO; watch recurring costs |
| Warranty | 1–3 years typical; negotiate on bulk |
| Customization | Branding, firmware, calibration |

## Vendors to Evaluate

**Industrial-grade:** Endress+Hauser, VEGA, Siemens, Emerson (Rosemount), Honeywell
**IoT-focused:** Milesight, Dragino, Netvox, Decentlab, Adeunis, Sensoneo
**Budget/China OEM:** YoLink, Gizwits, Seeed, Waveshare, Tuya-based

For bulk, Milesight (EM series), Dragino, and Netvox offer good price/performance for NB-IoT/LoRa tank monitoring.

## Hidden Costs to Model

1. **Platform/subscription** – per device per year ($5–50)
2. **SIM data plans** – $1–10/device/year
3. **Installation** – mounting, sealing, calibration
4. **Integration** – SCADA/API work
5. **Battery replacement** – labor often exceeds battery cost
6. **Certification for hazardous areas** – can 3–5x unit price

## Recommended Approach

1. **Define requirements** – tank type, depth range, accuracy (±1 cm vs ±1%), environment, hazardous rating
2. **Pilot 5–10 units** across 2–3 vendors for 60–90 days
3. **Compare TCO over 5 years**, not unit price
4. **Negotiate**: volume tiers, extended warranty, firmware customization, spare units
5. **Lock API access** – avoid vendor lock-in; require MQTT or REST

## Questions to Clarify

- How many tanks and what's the geographic spread?
- Hazardous area (fuel, chemicals) or benign (water, ag)?
- Accuracy needed – billing-grade or monitoring-only?
- Do you have existing LoRaWAN/cellular infrastructure?
- One-time capex vs. subscription model preference?
