Here are established suppliers for clamp-on ultrasonic flow meters suitable for process pipe retrofits, organized by tier and typical use case:

## Tier 1 — Major Process Instrumentation Brands

**Emerson (Flexim)**
- FLUXUS series (F601, F721, F731, G601, G721)
- Industry benchmark for clamp-on; strong for retrofit since no pipe cutting or shutdown needed
- Excellent for large pipes, high temperatures, and hazardous areas
- Wide range of transducer sizes and mounting options (including high-temperature and IP68)

**Endress+Hauser**
- Proline Prosonic Flow series (e.g., 91, 93, B, I, P, W)
- Strong integration with existing E+H process infrastructure
- Good for water/wastewater, chemical, and energy applications

**Krohne**
- OPTISONIC series (e.g., 6300, 6400, 7060)
- Well-regarded for accuracy and diagnostics
- Strong in oil & gas, chemical, and power

**Siemens**
- SITRANS FST020, FUP1010, FUS series
- Good for utility and industrial process retrofit; strong global service network

**Fuji Electric**
- Portaflow and Time Delta series
- Solid option for steam and high-temperature retrofits

## Tier 2 — Specialized / Value Clamp-On Suppliers

**Flexim (standalone)** — now part of Emerson, but still often specified independently

**Dynasonics (Badger Meter)** — good for water/wastewater retrofits, cost-effective

**Greyline Instruments** — PDFM series; budget-friendly for water/wastewater

**Micronics (UK)** — Portaflow, Ultraflo; strong in HVAC and building services

**Shenitech / Lanry / Acloud / Kacise (China)** — lower-cost alternatives; quality varies widely, but several are now used in OEM and utility retrofit projects

**Teledyne ISCO / Teledyne FLIR** — niche flow applications

## Key Selection Criteria for Pipe Retrofit

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Pipe material & liner | Affects transducer coupling and signal |
| Pipe OD & wall thickness | Must match transducer range |
| Fluid type & temperature | Determines transducer and coupling choice |
| Upstream/downstream straight run | Usually 10D/5D minimum |
| Permissible measurement uncertainty | 1–2% typical for clamp-on; 0.5% with multipath |
| Area classification (ATEX/IECEx) | Required for hazardous zones |
| Outputs / comms | 4–20 mA, HART, Modbus, Profibus, Ethernet |
| Power availability | Loop-powered vs. line-powered |

## Practical Recommendation

- **For critical process / high accuracy:** Emerson Flexim, KROHNE OPTISONIC, E+H Prosonic
- **For water/wastewater retrofit:** Badger Dynasonics, Greyline, Flexim
- **For budget retrofit with acceptable accuracy:** Micronics, Shenitech, Lanry
- **For steam / high temp:** Flexim G-series, Fuji
