For Indian process industries, an **80 GHz radar level transmitter** is a strong choice when you need accurate, non-contact level measurement in challenging applications such as chemicals, petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, water/wastewater, cement, power, and food processing. The higher frequency gives a narrower beam, better resolution, and easier installation compared to lower-frequency radar.

Here’s a practical guide tailored to the Indian market.

## Why 80 GHz Radar?

| Feature | Benefit |
|---|---|
| ~3–4° beam angle | Avoids obstacles, tank walls, agitators, internal pipes |
| Higher resolution | Measures low dielectric media (plastics, oils, some chemicals) |
| Wider dynamic range | Works in tall, narrow tanks and silos |
| Small antenna | Fits nozzles as small as 1½"–2" |
| Non-contact | Ideal for corrosive, hygienic, or high-temperature media |
| 2-wire / 4-wire options | Easy retrofit into existing loops |

## Typical Indian Applications

- **Chemical & petrochemical**: acids, solvents, hydrocarbons, resins
- **Pharmaceutical**: reactors, API storage, clean-in-place vessels
- **Water & wastewater**: sumps, clarifiers, wet wells, filter beds
- **Cement & mining**: clinker silos, fly ash, raw meal
- **Power**: DM water, fuel oil, ash hoppers
- **Food & beverage**: syrups, edible oils, dairy tanks
- **Steel & metals**: molten metal is not suitable, but ore bins, lime silos are

## Key Selection Criteria

1. **Process temperature & pressure**
   - Standard: up to 80–150 °C
   - High-temp versions: up to 200–450 °C with extended antenna
2. **Antenna material**
   - PTFE / PFA for chemicals
   - 316L SS for general industry
   - Hastelloy / Monel for corrosive service
3. **Output & protocol**
   - 4–20 mA + HART (most common in India)
   - Modbus RTU, Profibus PA, Foundation Fieldbus for DCS integration
4. **Approvals**
   - **PESO / CCOE** for hazardous areas (Flameproof / Ex d, IS / Ex ia)
   - **ATEX / IECEx** if imported
   - **BIS** compliance and CE marking
   - IP66/IP67 or IP68 for outdoor/wet wells
5. **Accuracy & range**
   - ±2–5 mm typical
   - Range up to 30–120 m depending on model

## Installation Tips for Indian Conditions

- **Avoid mounting over agitators or fill points**
- Use **stillwells** in turbulent tanks
- For **outdoor silos**, use sunshade to protect electronics from 45–50 °C summer heat
- Ensure **proper grounding** — critical in Indian plants with variable power quality
- Use **surge protection** for lightning-prone regions (coastal, central India)
- For **dusty solids**, choose lens antenna over horn
- Keep **nozzle length short**; 80 GHz tolerates some nozzle but not excessive

## When NOT to use 80 GHz

- Very low dielectric (<1.4) with heavy foam or condensation
- Interface measurement (oil–water) — consider guided wave radar (TDR)
- Extremely sticky or crystallizing media — use TDR or capacitance
- Very high pressure (>40 bar) — verify model rating

## Recommended Approach

1. Define media, dielectric constant, temperature, pressure, tank geometry

3. Shortlist 2–3 brands, request **datasheet + local service commitment**
4. Insist on **PESO/CCOE certificate** if in a classified zone
5. Ask for **on-site commissioning** — critical for radar echo mapping
