A **pressure transmitter for hydraulic systems** is a sensor that measures hydraulic fluid pressure and converts it into an electrical signal (typically 4–20 mA, 0–10 V, or digital like CAN/HART) for monitoring, control, or safety in hydraulic machinery.

## What It Does
- Measures **hydraulic pressure** (typically 0–350 bar / 0–5000 psi, up to 700+ bar in extreme cases)
- Sends a proportional electrical output to a PLC, controller, or display
- Enables closed-loop control, fault detection, and overload protection

## Key Specifications to Consider

| Parameter | Typical Range / Notes |
|---|---|
| Pressure range | 0–10 bar up to 0–1000 bar (choose ~1.5× max system pressure) |
| Output signal | 4–20 mA, 0–10 V, 0–5 V, ratiometric, CANbus, IO-Link |
| Accuracy | ±0.5% FS (standard) to ±0.1% FS (precision) |
| Operating temp | –40 °C to +125 °C (fluid & ambient) |
| Burst pressure | 3–4× rated range |
| Response time | 1–5 ms typical |
| Wetted materials | 17-4 PH stainless, 316L SS (must be hydraulic-fluid compatible) |
| Ingress protection | IP65–IP69K |
| Electrical connection | M12, DIN 43650, Deutsch, cable |

## Common Technologies
- **Strain gauge (thin-film / bonded)** — most common, robust, wide range
- **Piezoresistive MEMS** — compact, good for low-to-mid pressure
- **Capacitive** — high accuracy, good for low pressure
- **Piezoelectric** — dynamic measurements only (not steady-state)

## Typical Applications
- Mobile hydraulics (excavators, cranes, tractors)
- Industrial presses, injection molding machines
- Hydraulic power units (HPUs)
- Test benches and fluid power R&D
- Aerospace and marine actuation

## Selection Checklist
1. **Max system pressure** (including spikes/water hammer)
2. **Fluid compatibility** (mineral oil, water-glycol, phosphate ester, biodegradable)
3. **Output signal** required by your controller
4. **Environment** (IP rating, vibration, EMC)
5. **Process connection** (G1/4, M12×1.5, SAE, NPT, 7/16-20 UNF)
6. **Certifications** (ATEX, IECEx, UL, CE, DNV for marine)
7. **Shock & vibration** rating for mobile applications

## Installation Tips
- Mount away from high-turbulence areas (avoid pump discharge direct)
- Use a **snubber or dampener** where pressure spikes occur
- Ensure proper **grounding** to avoid EMI on mV-level signals
- Use **shielded cable** for 4–20 mA / voltage outputs
- For mobile hydraulics, choose models with **high burst and vibration ratings** (e.g., per ISO 16750)
