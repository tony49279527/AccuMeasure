For sea shipment of electromagnetic flow meters, proper packing is critical because these instruments are precision devices containing **coils, liners, electrodes, and electronic transmitters** that are sensitive to moisture, vibration, impact, and corrosion—especially during long ocean transit.

Here's a comprehensive packing guide:

## 1. Pre-Packing Preparation
- **Clean and dry** the flow tube interior; remove any residual liquid.
- **Cap/plug** both flanged ends with plastic or wooden covers to protect the liner (PTFE, rubber, PFA, etc.) and electrodes.
- **Short-circuit or protect signal terminals**; disconnect or secure the transmitter if separate.
- **Calibration certificate, wiring diagram, and manual** placed in a waterproof bag inside the crate.
- **Record serial numbers** and photograph the packed unit before closing.

## 2. Inner Protection
- Wrap the flow tube and transmitter separately in **PE film / VCI (Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor) film**.
- Add **silica gel desiccant** (typically 50–200 g per crate depending on volume).
- Use **foam (EPE/EPS), bubble wrap, or air pillows** to isolate the meter from the crate walls (min. 50 mm cushioning).
- For large diameter meters (DN200+), use **wooden saddles or cradles** to support the tube and prevent rolling.

## 3. Crating
- Use **export-grade plywood or heat-treated (ISPM 15) solid wood** crates — mandatory for most countries to prevent pest transfer.
- **Base:** sturdy pallet or skid rated for the weight (flow meters can be 20–500+ kg).
- **Fixing:** bolt or strap the meter to the crate base through the flanges or mounting holes; do not rely on foam alone.
- **Marking:** stencil or label:
  - "FRAGILE," "THIS SIDE UP," "KEEP DRY," "DO NOT STACK" (if applicable)
  - Gross/net weight, dimensions, case number
  - Consignee and shipping marks

## 4. Moisture & Corrosion Control
- Line the crate interior with **VCI film or aluminum foil barrier** for long voyages.
- Include **container desiccant bags** (e.g., 1–2 kg calcium chloride type) if shipping in an FCL container, especially for humid routes.
- Avoid direct contact between dissimilar metals (electrode terminals) and wood.

## 5. Container Loading
- **LCL:** insist on "no stacking" or place on top of the stow.
- **FCL:** block and brace the crate to prevent shifting; use airbags or lashing.
- Keep away from container doors and moisture-prone areas.
- Consider **shock/vibration indicators** (e.g., ShockWatch) for high-value meters.

## 6. Documentation
- Commercial invoice, packing list (with weights/dimensions per case)
- Certificate of origin, HS code (typically **9026.10** for flow meters)
- **ISPM 15 stamp** on wood packaging
- Insurance certificate (recommend all-risk marine cargo insurance)

## 7. Special Considerations
| Factor | Action |
|---|---|
| **PTFE/PFA liner** | Extra care — brittle if impacted; avoid sharp edges |
| **Rubber liner** | Protect from ozone/UV; keep sealed |
| **Hazardous area (Ex) units** | Keep certification labels visible; do not alter |
| **Separate transmitter** | Pack in its own foam-lined carton inside the crate |
| **Long transit (>30 days)** | Add extra desiccant, use VCI, consider desiccant-lined container |

## Recommended Packing Sequence
1. Clean → plug → wrap in VCI film
2. Foam cushion → place in cradle/saddle
3. Bolt to crate base
4. Add desiccant → seal with barrier film
5. Close crate → apply markings → strap
6. Load with bracing → document → insure
