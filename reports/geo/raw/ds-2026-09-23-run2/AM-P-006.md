I can help you find wholesale digital pressure gauges with custom branding. Here's what you need to know:

## Key Specs to Define First
- **Pressure range**: 0-100 psi, 0-1000 psi, 0-10,000 psi, etc.
- **Accuracy**: ±0.5%, ±0.25%, ±0.1% FS
- **Type**: Battery-powered, loop-powered (4-20mA), or wireless
- **Connection**: 1/4" NPT, 1/2" NPT, BSP, or metric threads
- **Certifications**: CE, RoHS, ATEX (if hazardous areas), NSF (if food/water)

## Common Wholesale Sources

**China (most competitive for OEM/private label):**
- **Alibaba / Made-in-China**: Search "digital pressure gauge OEM" — MOQs often 50-500 pcs
- **Shenzhen/Hangzhou manufacturers**: Many specialize in private label gauges
- **Typical pricing**: $8-25/unit at 500+ pcs depending on specs

**Taiwan/Korea:** Higher quality, MOQ 100-500 pcs, $20-60/unit

**US/EU distributors with OEM programs:**
- Winters Instruments, Ashcroft, Noshok, WIKA — higher cost but faster lead times and easier compliance

## Custom Branding Options
1. **Laser etching** on the faceplate/bezel (most common)
2. **Pad printing / silk screen** on the housing
3. **Custom LCD boot logo** (some models)
4. **Custom packaging** (box, blister, manual)
5. **Custom color** housing/trim rings

**Typical setup fees**: $50-300 per logo/laser die
**Lead time**: 15-35 days after sample approval

## Questions to Ask Suppliers
- What's the actual MOQ for custom branding (not just stock units)?
- IP rating (IP65/IP67 for washdown)?
- Battery life and type (AAA, coin cell)?
- Are calibration certs included?
- Warranty and DOA policy?
- Can they do private-label packaging?
