Radar level sensors are a popular choice for measuring solids in silos because they offer **non-contact measurement**, high accuracy, and minimal maintenance compared to mechanical systems. Here's a comprehensive overview:

## How Radar Level Sensors Work

Radar sensors emit microwave pulses (or continuous FMCW signals) from an antenna at the top of the silo. The signal reflects off the material surface, and the time-of-flight (or frequency shift) determines the distance, which is converted to level.

## Types for Solids

| Type | Frequency | Best For |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Pulse radar (non-contact)** | 6–80 GHz | Most silos, dusty environments |
| **FMCW radar** | 24–80 GHz | High accuracy, short-to-medium range |
| **Guided wave radar (TDR)** | ~1 GHz | Clean solids, liquids; less common for bulk solids |

For silos, **low-frequency pulse radar (6–26 GHz)** with a large horn or parabolic antenna is often preferred because it penetrates dust better.

## Key Advantages for Solids

- **No contact** → no wear from abrasive materials (cement, sand, grain, pellets)
- **Unaffected by dust, temperature, pressure, or vapor**
- **Works with changing dielectric constant** (unlike capacitive sensors)
- **Long measurement range** (up to 100 m in large silos)
- **Low maintenance** – no mechanical parts

## Challenges & Solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| **Dust clouds** | Use low-frequency (6–10 GHz) + high gain antenna |
| **Angled/uneven surface** | Aim antenna vertically; use software averaging |
| **Low dielectric material** (plastic pellets, fly ash) | Higher frequency, larger antenna, or guided wave |
| **Build-up on antenna** | Air purge, PTFE-coated antenna, or horn design |
| **Condensation** | Heated or purged antenna |
| **Multiple reflections** | Advanced echo processing algorithms |

## Typical Specifications

- **Frequency:** 6.3 GHz, 26 GHz, or 80 GHz
- **Range:** up to 100 m (depends on antenna)
- **Accuracy:** ±3 mm to ±15 mm
- **Beam angle:** 4°–20° (narrower = better for tall silos)
- **Process temp:** -40 °C to +200 °C (higher with extensions)
- **Output:** 4–20 mA / HART, Modbus, Profibus

## Common Applications

- Cement and clinker silos
- Grain and feed storage
- Plastic pellets and resins
- Fly ash and coal
- Sand, aggregates, minerals
- Wood chips, biomass

## Installation Tips

1. Mount away from the fill stream and walls
2. Keep a clear, unobstructed path to the material surface
3. Account for the **blocking distance** (dead zone near antenna)
4. Use a **still pipe** if turbulence/dust is severe
5. Consider antenna **purge** for sticky powders
6. Verify with **empty and full calibration**
