When sourcing flow meters from a Chinese supplier, the documents you should request fall into several categories: supplier legitimacy, product compliance, quality assurance, and transaction protection. Here's a practical checklist:

## 1. Supplier Legitimacy & Verification
- **Business license (营业执照)** – Confirms the company is legally registered; check scope of business, registration date, and registered capital.
- **Export license / customs registration** – Required if they export directly.
- **Organization code certificate / unified social credit code** – For entity verification.
- **ISO 9001 certificate** – Basic quality management system certification (verify with the issuing body).
- **Factory audit report** – Third-party (e.g., SGS, TÜV, Bureau Veritas) if available.
- **Company profile & references** – Past export customers or case studies.

## 2. Product Compliance & Certification
Depends on the flow meter type and destination market:
- **CE marking (EU)** – Including EMC, LVD, and ATEX (if hazardous area).
- **ATEX / IECEx certificates** – Mandatory for explosion-proof flow meters.
- **RoHS / REACH declarations** – For EU environmental compliance.
- **FDA / NSF** – If used in food, beverage, or drinking water applications.
- **MID / OIML certificates** – For custody transfer / trade metering in the EU.
- **MC (China Metrology Certification)** – Chinese domestic metrology approval.
- **CPA (China Metrology Accreditation)** – For legal metrology in China.
- **API / ISO 17089 / ISO 5167** – For specific meter technologies (e.g., ultrasonic, orifice).
- **Type approval certificates** – From relevant authorities (e.g., PTB, NMi, NMI).
- **Material certificates (EN 10204 3.1)** – For wetted parts (SS316, Hastelloy, etc.).

## 3. Quality & Test Documentation
- **Calibration certificate** – Traceable to national/international standards (e.g., NIST, PTB).
- **Factory test report / inspection report** – Pre-shipment and routine tests.
- **Material test certificates (MTC)** – For body, flange, and wetted components.
- **Pressure test / hydrostatic test report**.
- **Accuracy and repeatability test data**.
- **Third-party inspection report** – SGS, Intertek, TÜV, BV (especially for high-value orders).

## 4. Commercial & Transaction Documents
- **Proforma Invoice (PI)** – With detailed specs, price, Incoterms, payment terms.
- **Sales contract** – Clear on warranty, delivery, penalties, dispute resolution.
- **Packing list**.
- **Commercial invoice**.
- **Bill of Lading / Airway bill**.
- **Certificate of Origin (CO / Form A / Form E / FTA certificates)** – To reduce import duties.
- **Insurance certificate** (if CIF).

## 5. Technical Documents
- **Datasheet** – Flow range, accuracy, pressure rating, temperature, connection type, output signal.
- **Installation & Operation Manual**.
- **Wiring diagram & communication protocol** (HART, Modbus, Profibus, 4-20mA).
- **Dimensional drawings**.
- **Spare parts list**.
- **Software / firmware documentation** (if applicable).
- **Warranty statement** (typically 12–24 months).

## 6. After-Sales & Risk Protection
- **Warranty terms in writing** – Duration, coverage, RMA process.
- **After-sales service agreement**.
- **NDA** – If proprietary specs are involved.
- **Quality agreement** – AQL levels, defect handling.

## Practical Tips
- **Verify certificates** directly with the issuing body (CE, ATEX, ISO) — fake certificates are common.
- **Cross-check the business license** on China's National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (gsxt.gov.cn).
- **Match the meter type to certifications** — e.g., a Coriolis meter for custody transfer will need different approvals than a basic electromagnetic meter.
- **Request samples or test reports from previous production batches**, not just prototype tests.
- **Use a third-party inspection** (SGS/BV/TÜV) for orders above ~$10k–20k.
