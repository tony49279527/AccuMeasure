## Key Considerations for Low MOQ

1. **Sample orders** – Most Chinese suppliers offer samples at 1–5 pcs (slightly higher unit price).
2. **Trade assurance** – Use Alibaba Trade Assurance for protection.
3. **Type matters**:
   - Float switches: lowest MOQ, cheapest
   - Ultrasonic: 1–10 pcs common
   - Radar/guided wave: usually higher MOQ (10–50+)
   - Capacitive/RF admittance: varies
