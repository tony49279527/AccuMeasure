For liquid level sensors (float, ultrasonic, radar, capacitance, etc.) sourced from China, here are typical lead times and sea freight estimates:

## Production Lead Time (China)

| Order Type | Typical Lead Time |
|---|---|
| Samples / small qty (<100 pcs) | 5–10 days |
| Standard models, 100–1,000 pcs | 15–25 days |
| Custom/OEM (branding, specs) | 25–40 days |
| Large volume (5,000+ pcs) | 30–45 days |
| Radar/ultrasonic (complex) | 30–50 days |

**Add:** Chinese New Year (2–4 weeks shutdown), National Day (1 week), and buffer for component shortages.

## Sea Freight from China

**Transit times (port-to-port):**

| Route | Transit Time |
|---|---|
| China → US West Coast | 14–22 days |
| China → US East Coast | 25–35 days |
| China → North Europe | 25–35 days |
| China → Mediterranean | 22–30 days |
| China → Australia | 12–20 days |
| China → Middle East | 12–20 days |

**Add:** 3–7 days for booking + origin handling, 2–5 days destination clearance + delivery.

**Indicative costs (2025, LCL/FCL):**

- **LCL:** ~$80–180 per CBM (min 1 CBM), plus fees
- **FCL 20ft:** $1,200–3,500 depending on route/season
- **FCL 40ft:** $1,800–5,000

Rates spike 30–100% during peak season (Aug–Oct) and pre-CNY.

## Total Door-to-Door (Example)

China → US West Coast, 500 pcs standard float sensors:
- Production: ~20 days
- Booking + origin: ~5 days
- Sea transit: ~18 days
- Clearance + delivery: ~5 days
- **Total: ~45–50 days**

## Tips to Reduce Time

1. **Choose express (DHL/FedEx)** for <100 kg — 3–7 days, though costly ($5–10/kg).
2. **Air freight** for 100–500 kg — 5–10 days, ~$4–8/kg.
3. **Book sea freight 2–3 weeks before production finishes.**
4. **Use a freight forwarder** for consolidated LCL if under 15 CBM.
5. **Negotiate ex-works vs FOB vs DDP** — DDP simplifies but costs more.
