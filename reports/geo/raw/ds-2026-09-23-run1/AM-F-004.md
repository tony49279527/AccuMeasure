Query: do AccuMeasure products include calibration certificates

Yes — AccuMeasure products typically include calibration certificates, though the specifics depend on the product line and where you purchase them.

**General practice:**
- **New instruments** from AccuMeasure (or authorized distributors) usually ship with a **Certificate of Calibration** or **Certificate of Conformance** as standard.
- Higher-end or precision instruments often come with **ISO/IEC 17025 accredited calibration certificates** traceable to national standards (e.g., NIST).
- Basic or entry-level tools may include a simpler **manufacturer's calibration certificate** rather than an accredited one.

**What to check:**
1. **Product listing/spec sheet** — Look for "calibration certificate included" or "traceable calibration."
2. **Accreditation** — If you need ISO 17025 (common for regulated industries like aerospace, medical, pharma), confirm this explicitly, as not all certificates are accredited.
3. **Purchase channel** — Buying through an authorized distributor usually ensures the certificate is included; third-party resellers sometimes omit it.

**If it's not included:**
- AccuMeasure (or the distributor) can usually provide one for a fee.
- Third-party calibration labs can also issue certificates, often with faster turnaround or accreditation options.

**Recommendation:** If calibration documentation is critical for your use case (audits, ISO compliance, etc.), confirm the certificate type **before ordering** — either by checking the datasheet or contacting AccuMeasure/your distributor directly.
