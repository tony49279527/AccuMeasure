For Middle East oil & gas projects, radar level transmitters are a core instrument choice — but the region's extreme conditions (high ambient temps, heavy H₂S/sour service, sand, large tank farms) mean selection goes well beyond a generic datasheet. Here's a practical overview.

## Why radar dominates in ME oil & gas

- **Non-contacting** → no wetted moving parts, ideal for corrosive/sour hydrocarbons
- **High accuracy** (±1–3 mm) for custody transfer and inventory
- **Works with vapors, foam, and pressure** better than DP or float gauges
- **Low maintenance** — critical when sites are remote (Rub' al Khali, Western Desert, offshore Gulf)

## Key selection criteria for ME environment

1. **Ambient temperature** — 50–55 °C typical, up to 65 °C in shade; many transmitters derate above 55 °C. Look for units rated to 70–80 °C or use remote-mount electronics.
2. **Process temperature** — 100–150 °C in heavy crude; high-temp versions needed.
3. **Pressure** — separators up to 100 bar; HP wells higher.
4. **H₂S / sour service** — NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 compliance; wetted parts in 316L SS, Hastelloy C-276, or PTFE-lined.
5. **Sand/dust ingress** — IP66/IP67 minimum, IP68 for submerged.
6. **Hazardous area** — IECEx / ATEX / NEC; Ex ia or Ex d; often SIL 2/3 per IEC 61508.
7. **Communication** — HART 7, Foundation Fieldbus, Profibus PA, or Modbus; increasingly Ethernet-APL.
8. **Antenna choice** — lens, horn, or parabolic; encapsulated lens best for condensation and buildup.

## Standards & approvals to specify

- **API MPMS Ch. 3.1B / OIML R85** for custody transfer tanks
- **IEC 61508 SIL 2/3** for overfill protection (API 2350)
- **IECEx / ATEX / NEC** for hazardous areas
- **NACE MR0175** for sour service
- **IEC 60529** IP66/67/68
- **API 2350** overfill prevention (critical in ME tank farms)

## Project-specific tips

- **ADNOC, Saudi Aramco, QatarEnergy, KOC, PDO** each have approved vendor lists (AVL) — check the client's spec before proposing brands.
- **Saudi Aramco** typically requires 80 GHz for new tank gauging; strict on IECEx and SIL.
- **ADNOC** favors Foundation Fieldbus in newer greenfield work.
- For **remote desert sites**, choose 2-wire loop-powered with HART for minimum wiring, or wirelessHART for brownfield retrofits.
- **Commissioning** — allow for empty-tank spectrum mapping; sand and condensation can create false echoes.
- **Spare parts** — ME projects usually require 2-year operating spares + critical spares list.
