## Selection Criteria for Industrial Tank Inventory

**1. Measurement principle**
- 80GHz FMCW radar = narrow beam (~3–4°), ideal for tanks with nozzles, agitators, foam, or condensation
- Better than 26GHz for small tanks, tall nozzles, and low-dielectric media (εr ≥ 1.5)

**2. Accuracy & range**
- Typical: ±1–3 mm for inventory; ±0.5 mm for custody transfer
- Range: 0–30 m typical, up to 120 m for silos

**3. Process conditions**
- Temperature: -40 to +200°C (some to +450°C with extension)
- Pressure: up to 40 bar (higher for special models)
- Antenna: PTFE, PEEK, or ceramic for corrosive media

**4. Outputs / Integration**
- 4–20 mA + HART, Modbus RTU, Profibus PA, Foundation Fieldbus, Ethernet/IP
- For tank inventory: look for **multi-tank software** (e.g., E+H Inventory Management, Emerson TankMaster, Honeywell Enraf)

**5. Certifications**
- ATEX/IECEx, FM, CSA (hazardous areas)
- SIL 2/3 for safety loops
- OIML R85 / API MPMS Ch. 3.1B for custody transfer

**6. Environmental**
- IP66/IP67/IP68, NEMA 4X
- Suitable for outdoor, condensation, dust
