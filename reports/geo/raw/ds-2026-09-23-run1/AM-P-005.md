Here's a comprehensive guide for placing an OEM order for pressure transmitters with your private label:

## Key Steps for OEM Private Label Order

### 1. Define Your Specifications
**Technical Parameters:**
- Pressure range (e.g., 0-100 bar, 0-1000 psi)
- Output signal (4-20mA, 0-10V, HART, RS485/Modbus)
- Accuracy class (±0.5%, ±0.25%, ±0.1% FS)
- Process connection (G1/4, 1/2"NPT, M20×1.5, flange)
- Electrical connection (DIN 43650, M12, cable)
- Wetted materials (316L SS, Hastelloy, ceramic)
- Operating temperature range
- IP rating (IP65, IP67, IP68)
- Certifications (CE, ATEX, IECEx, UL, RoHS)

**Application:** HVAC, water treatment, hydraulics, oil & gas, food & beverage, automotive

### 2. Private Label Requirements
- **Branding:** Laser engraving, silk-screen, or label with your logo
- **Packaging:** Custom boxes, manuals, certificates with your brand
- **Product code:** Your own model numbering system
- **Warranty card:** Under your company name

### 3. MOQ & Pricing Tiers (Typical)
| Quantity | Unit Price Impact |
|----------|------------------|
| 50-100 pcs | Sample/low volume premium |
| 500-1,000 pcs | 15-25% lower |
| 5,000+ pcs | Best pricing, tooling amortized |

Expect **MOQ 100-500 pcs** for most Chinese/Taiwanese OEM factories; European OEMs typically require 500-2,000 pcs.

### 4. Supplier Evaluation Checklist
- ISO 9001 / IATF 16949 certification
- Existing OEM clients (references)
- In-house calibration & testing lab
- Lead time (typically 4-8 weeks)
- Tooling/mold cost for custom housing
- Prototype lead time (2-4 weeks)
- Payment terms (30% deposit, 70% before shipment typical)
- After-sales & RMA policy

### 5. Order Process
1. **RFQ** → send specs to 3-5 suppliers
2. **Sample order** → validate performance, branding, packaging
3. **Pilot run** → 100-500 pcs to test market
4. **Mass production** → sign contract, tooling, production
5. **QC inspection** → third-party (SGS, BV) or in-house
6. **Shipping** → FOB/CIF/DDP terms

### 6. Costs to Budget
- **Tooling/mold:** $500-$5,000 (one-time, if custom housing)
- **Logo printing setup:** $100-$500
- **Certification (own brand):** $2,000-$10,000 (CE/ATEX)
- **Sample fee:** Usually refundable on mass order
- **Packaging design:** $200-$1,000

### 7. Recommended OEM Sources
- **China:** WNK, Senex, Yunyi, Supmea (cost-effective, good quality)
- **Taiwan:** ADI, JSP, E+H partners
- **Europe:** BD Sensors, Keller, Gefran (premium price)
- **USA:** Ashcroft, SSI Technologies

### 8. Contract Must-Haves
- IP ownership of your brand/tooling
- Confidentiality (NDA)
- Quality guarantee & penalty clause
- Spare parts availability (5-10 years)
- Exclusivity clause (if desired)
