Here are some established piezoresistive pressure transmitter manufacturers for industrial equipment, along with what they're known for:

## Global Leaders

**WIKA** (Germany) – One of the broadest industrial portfolios; piezoresistive transmitters like the S-10, S-11, and A-10 series. Strong in process, OEM, and hazardous-area applications.

**Endress+Hauser** (Switzerland) – Premium process instrumentation; Deltabar and Cerabar families. Excellent for chemical, pharmaceutical, and food/beverage.

**Emerson (Rosemount)** (USA) – Rosemount 2088, 3051, and 4600 series. Industry standard for process control, oil & gas, and refining.

**Yokogawa** (Japan) – EJA-E series. High accuracy and stability for process automation.

**AB罗克韦尔 / Allen-Bradley** – Integrated with factory automation and PLC ecosystems.

**Siemens** – Sitrans P series; strong where Siemens automation is already deployed.

**Honeywell** – STG/STD series; process and aerospace heritage.

## Mid-Range / Value-Oriented

**BD Sensors** (Germany) – Good price/performance for OEM and industrial machinery.

**Keller** (Switzerland) – High-accuracy piezoresistive sensors; popular in test benches and demanding OEM work.

**Huba Control** (Switzerland) – Compact transmitters for HVAC, pneumatics, and industrial equipment.

**Druck (Baker Hughes)** – Known for high-accuracy and harsh-environment transmitters.

## Asian Manufacturers (Cost-Competitive)

**Yudian, Supmea, Meacon, Binzhou Puxin** (China) – Widely used for general industrial equipment; verify certifications if you need ATEX/IECEx or SIL.

**Autonics, TURCK** – More sensor/automation focused, but offer pressure transmitters.

## Selection Criteria for Industrial Equipment

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Accuracy (% FS or % span) | 0.25%, 0.5%, 1% typical tiers |
| Pressure range | Match to system max + safety margin |
| Output signal | 4–20 mA, 0–10 V, HART, RS-485, IO-Link |
| Media compatibility | Wetted materials (316L SS, Hastelloy, ceramics) |
| Process connection | G¼, G½, NPT, hygienic (Tri-Clamp, DIN) |
| Certifications | ATEX, IECEx, SIL 2/3, CE, UL |
| Temperature range | Compensated range vs. operating range |
| Ingress protection | IP65, IP67, IP68 |

## Practical Recommendation

- **High-end process / hazardous area** → Endress+Hauser, Rosemount, Yokogawa, WIKA
- **OEM machinery / cost-sensitive** → BD Sensors, Huba, Keller, Supmea
- **Factory automation integration** → Siemens, Rockwell, ifm (with IO-Link)
