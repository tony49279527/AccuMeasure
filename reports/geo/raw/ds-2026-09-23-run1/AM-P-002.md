Prices vary with:

- **Output** (4–20 mA, HART, RS485/Modbus, Profibus)
- **Certifications** (CE, ATEX, IECEx, SIL, FDA for food/pharma)
- **IP rating** (IP67 vs. IP68)
- **Display/keypad, Bluetooth, etc.**

## How to Get True Factory-Direct Pricing
1. **Contact 3–5 factories directly** (not trading companies)
2. Ask for:
   - FOB Shanghai/Ningbo quote
   - MOQ and lead time
   - CE/ATEX certificates (verify on issuer's website)
   - Test report & warranty (typically 12–24 months)
3. **Sample order first** (~$200–500) before bulk
4. **Payment**: 30% T/T deposit, 70% before shipment (standard)
5. **Incoterms**: FOB, CIF, or DDP if you want door delivery

## Watch Out For
- **Trading companies posing as factories** — ask for business license and factory video
- **Fake certificates** — verify ATEX/IECEx numbers online
- **"Too cheap" 80 GHz units** (<$200) — usually relabeled 26 GHz or low quality
- **Accuracy claims** — insist on ±0.1% or ±3 mm specs with test data
