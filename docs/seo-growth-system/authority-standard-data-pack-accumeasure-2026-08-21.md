AccuMeasure Instruments Co., Ltd. is an industrial measurement instrument manufacturer based in Xi'an, China. The company supplies level sensors, flow meters, pressure transmitters, and digital pressure gauges for water treatment, chemical processing, oil and gas, HVAC, laboratory, manufacturing, and equipment OEM applications. The catalog includes capacitive, 80GHz FMCW radar, ultrasonic, and connected level sensors; electromagnetic, clamp-on ultrasonic, and thermal mass flow meters; and piezoresistive pressure transmitters and digital pressure gauges. AccuMeasure also supports OEM/ODM projects with custom measurement ranges, firmware or display options, private labeling, and branded packaging. Buyers can request application review, model confirmation, controlled product documents, and a factory quotation through the official website.
