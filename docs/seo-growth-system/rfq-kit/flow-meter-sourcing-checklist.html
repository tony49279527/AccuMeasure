First-pass technology notes

  • Electromagnetic meters generally require a suitable conductive liquid and a full pipe.
  • Clamp-on ultrasonic meters can be considered when external installation or avoiding shutdown is important; confirm pipe and acoustic conditions.
  • Thermal mass meters are evaluated for gas service; confirm gas composition, pressure, temperature, and calibration requirements.

These are early project-definition rules. Final model selection, operating limits, certificate scope, calibration requirements, and commercial terms must be confirmed against current controlled documents and the quotation.
