# Flow Meter Sourcing Checklist Pilot

## Pilot decision

**Selected asset:** `flow-meter-sourcing-checklist.md`  
**Primary buyers:** water-treatment procurement teams, plant engineers, system integrators, retrofit contractors, and importers  
**Primary product paths:** `/products/flow`, `/applications/electromagnetic-flow-meter-for-water-treatment`, `/compare/electromagnetic-vs-ultrasonic-flow-meter`, `/contact`  
**Pilot length:** 60-90 days after approved distribution  
**Current status:** local preparation only; no partner contacted

## Why this asset is suitable

The checklist addresses information buyers need before asking for a quote: fluid properties, conductivity, pipe size, lining, flange, installation access, shutdown constraints, signal, and documentation. It is useful even when the buyer does not choose AccuMeasure, which gives an association, directory, distributor, or integrator a legitimate reason to share it.

## Partner-facing offer

### Short description

> A practical flow meter sourcing checklist for industrial and water-treatment projects. It helps buyers collect fluid, pipe, installation, signal, and documentation requirements before comparing electromagnetic, clamp-on ultrasonic, or thermal mass flow meter options.

### What the partner receives

- A neutral buyer-facing checklist;
- A co-branded or partner-introduction version after approval;
- A clear route for buyers who need an engineering review;
- A source record for downloads and qualified RFQs.

### What AccuMeasure requests

- Permission to place the resource in a relevant buyer-resource page, event-resource page, member resource page, or supplier guide;
- A descriptive reference to the resource and its intended audience;
- Accurate attribution and a normal link when the partner considers it useful.

There is no fixed anchor text, reciprocal-link requirement, paid-link requirement, or promise of a dofollow link.

## Distribution variants

### Association or directory

Use the neutral checklist with the partner's resource-page introduction. Do not add unapproved certificate, customer, factory, or performance claims.

### Distributor or system integrator

Use a co-branded version with a short note explaining the distributor/integrator's service scope. The document must identify who handles the inquiry and must not imply a partnership before written approval.

### Industrial media or trade show

Use the checklist as a pre-RFQ guide or event download. Sponsored placement, advertising, or booth packages require separate cost approval and are not included in the pilot by default.

## Recommended call to action

> Complete the checklist before requesting a quotation. For a project review, send the pipe, fluid, installation, and documentation requirements through the supplier's RFQ path.

The public destination, once approved for publication, should be one canonical AccuMeasure URL with a stable page title and a clear RFQ path. No separate duplicate landing pages should be created for every partner.

## Evidence and compliance gate

Before external release, Owner must approve:

- The product categories and technology descriptions;
- Any numeric performance, accuracy, range, conductivity, material, or certification statement;
- The final destination URL and form fields;
- The named RFI owner and response SLA;
- Whether partner branding or a partner relationship may be displayed;
- Privacy, download, and lead attribution language.

Do not include customer names, order quantities, savings figures, certificate numbers, or factory statistics unless the evidence matrix is approved for public use.

## Pilot success criteria

### Minimum viable signal

- One relevant partner accepts the resource for review;
- One approved publication or resource-page placement;
- At least one referred buyer reaches a flow product or application page;
- At least one completed RFQ contains usable fluid and pipe data.

### Strong signal

- Multiple relevant partners use the checklist independently;
- Referred buyers have procurement or engineering intent;
- At least one qualified inquiry can be attributed to the resource;
- The partner renews or expands the resource placement without being asked for a backlink.

### Do not count

- A directory entry with no buyer activity;
- A paid link with no relevant audience;
- Search-engine index counts as a proxy for leads;
- Unverified brand mentions or scraped profiles.

## Execution checklist

1. Owner approves the checklist and evidence boundary.
2. Owner approves one pilot channel and a single responsible contact.
3. Prepare a final PDF/HTML version from the local draft.
4. Record the canonical destination and UTM convention.
5. Obtain explicit authorization before any external contact or submission.
6. Record the partner response and placement URL without guessing outcome.
7. Review referral visits, RFQ starts, completed RFQs, qualified inquiries, and response time after 30, 60, and 90 days.
