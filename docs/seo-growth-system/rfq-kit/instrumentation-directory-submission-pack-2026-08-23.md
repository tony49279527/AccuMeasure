## Verified channel facts

- The directory describes itself as a resource for measurement and sensor companies.
- Bronze includes a simple company description, logo, and 10 product categories online.
- Silver adds social details and a 120-word company blurb in the printed contact directory.
- Gold adds an HTML company description, 15 product categories, a highlighted profile, and two extra file uploads.
- Platinum adds top-of-category placement, 20 product categories, a highlighted profile, and four extra file uploads.
- The official page says applicants can register or contact the directory team; prices are not shown publicly.
- The Contact page requests name, email, company, message, and a package selection. It includes a human-check field.
- The official pages do not establish that AccuMeasure is already listed, that a free backlink is available, or that a package will generate qualified inquiries.

## Recommended package decision

Do not choose a package before price, term, renewal, editorial review, public profile URL, website-link treatment, file-upload rules, lead routing, and cancellation terms are confirmed in writing.

For a first test, ask for Bronze and Silver pricing and placement details only. Gold and Platinum add visibility and files but increase cost risk; they are not justified before buyer traffic is proven.
