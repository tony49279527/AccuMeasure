## 6. Buyer decision guide

- Electromagnetic flow meters generally require a suitable conductive liquid and a full pipe; confirm conductivity and installation conditions.
- Clamp-on ultrasonic meters can be considered when external installation or avoiding pipe shutdown is important; confirm pipe material, wall condition, lining, and acoustic path.
- Thermal mass meters are evaluated for gas service; confirm gas composition, pressure, temperature, and calibration requirements.

These are first-pass selection rules. The quoted model, limits, compatibility, certificate scope, and commercial terms must be confirmed against the current controlled product documents.
