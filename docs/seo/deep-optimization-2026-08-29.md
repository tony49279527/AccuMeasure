## Implemented Changes

- Removed public fixed response-time, factory-direct, factory-location, and unsupported manufacturer language from conversion paths and metadata.
- Reframed the flow-meter and industry pages around selection conditions, RFQ inputs, and document verification rather than unsupported certification or performance promises.
- Removed the Product schema manufacturer relationship. Product schema retains the visible product name, description, image, brand, URL, category, and visible selection properties.
- Replaced exposed hazardous-area certification labels with explicit current-document checks. Product certification arrays are empty until approved evidence is available.
- Added a minimal client product directory for navigation, product search, and contact prefill. The full product data set no longer ships to those client components, reducing unnecessary hydration data and preventing non-rendered commercial or compliance fields from entering their browser bundles.
- Updated sitemap last-modified dates to the actual release date.

## Measurement Plan

- After production deployment, validate HTML, canonical, title, H1, Product and Breadcrumb structured data, sitemap, robots, llms.txt, product redirects, and inquiry failure behavior.

- Do not install analytics, consent tooling, pixels, or third-party lead systems without explicit owner approval.

## Evidence Still Required

- Original ISO 9001, CE, ATEX, RoHS, IECEx, and FCC records with issuer, exact model scope, validity, and publication approval.
- Legal entity and manufacturing-scope confirmation for company and location claims.
- Approved QC, calibration, traceability, test, factory, capacity, export-market, customer, order, lead-time, and outcome evidence.
- RFQ owner, escalation path, delivery configuration, and response-SLA approval.
